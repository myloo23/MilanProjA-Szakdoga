The deck quotes **37s**: when measurements disagree the honest single figure is
the worst one, and it is also the freshest. Two identical runs and then three
seconds of drift is a machine under slightly different load, not a broken
measurement — but it does mean the claim is "repeats to within a few seconds",
not "identical". The slide says exactly that, because the alternative is
claiming precision this rig does not have.

## Found while capturing these

The error-rate panel was drawing **green across a 96% spike**. Three causes, all
in `app-golden-signals.json`, all fixed on 2026-08-11:

1. `color.mode` was `palette-classic`, so the line took its colour from the
   palette and was green at any value. The thresholds were defined and unused.
2. `thresholdsStyle` was absent, so the limit was never drawn on the graph.
3. The red step sat at **0.2** while the alert rule fires at **0.1** — the
   dashboard and the alert disagreed about what counts as bad.

A fourth followed from the fix: with `color.mode: thresholds` alone, Grafana
colours the whole series by its most recent point, which answers "is it bad
right now" rather than "when was it bad". `gradientMode: scheme` makes the
colour follow the value along the line.

None of this was found by reading the dashboard. It was found by going to
photograph a thing that had already been marked done — which is the concrete
version of the claim on the "what I learned" slide, and worth saying in those
words if it comes up.
